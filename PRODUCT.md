# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Minecraft players and modpack creators, ranging from beginners who need clear guidance to experienced users who want a fast, compact workflow. They use Dynrinth to find compatible content, resolve dependencies, assemble a queue, and export or share a ready-to-use pack.

## Product Purpose

Dynrinth brings discovery, compatibility filtering, dependency resolution, and bulk downloading for multiple Minecraft content sources into one web tool. Success means a beginner can complete the flow without prior ecosystem knowledge while an experienced user can configure and export a pack without unnecessary friction.

## Brand Personality

Technical, direct, and trustworthy. The interface should feel capable and approachable rather than simplistic, exposing advanced controls progressively without hiding useful information from experienced users.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS-dashboard styling, decorative visual noise, novelty interactions, and layouts that prioritize presentation over the download workflow. Do not make the product feel intimidating to beginners or artificially limited for expert users.

## Design Principles

- Make the primary search-to-download path obvious on first use.
- Reveal complexity progressively while keeping advanced controls close at hand.
- Prefer familiar, predictable controls and immediate state feedback.
- Keep information dense enough for expert use without sacrificing legibility.
- Treat compatibility, errors, and dependency decisions as user-facing guidance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation and visible focus states, maintain sufficient color contrast, avoid color-only meaning, respect reduced-motion preferences, and keep touch interactions usable on small screens.
